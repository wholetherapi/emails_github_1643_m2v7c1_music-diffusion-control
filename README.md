## Paper Information

- **Title**: Controllable Diffusion Models for Symbolic Music Generation
- **Status**: Accepted at ACM MM 2026 - ACM International Conference on Multimedia
- **Authors**: Patrick Carter, et al.

## Abstract

We study fine-grained conditioning of diffusion models for symbolic music,
enabling control over instrumentation, tempo and harmonic structure while
preserving musical coherence.
